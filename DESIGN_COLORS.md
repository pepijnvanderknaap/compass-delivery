# Compass Delivery - Color Palette

## Primary Colors

### Micro-blue
**Hex:** `#0078D4`
**Usage:** Icons, Sign Out button background, interactive elements
**Hover:** `#106EBE`

### Off-black
**Hex:** `#1D1D1F`
**Usage:** Primary text, headings, titles, primary icons (non-interactive)
**Notes:** Apple's near-black for maximum readability

## Neutral Grays

### Medium Gray
**Hex:** `#6E6E73`
**Usage:** Secondary text, subtitles, descriptions, body text

### Light Gray (Tertiary)
**Hex:** `#86868B`
**Usage:** Tertiary text, disabled states, placeholder text

### Light Gray (Borders)
**Hex:** `#D2D2D7`
**Usage:** Standard borders, dividers, card outlines

### Ultra Light Gray (Borders)
**Hex:** `#E8E8ED`
**Usage:** Subtle borders, light dividers, disabled borders

### Background Gray
**Hex:** `#FAFAFA`
**Usage:** Page backgrounds, subtle section backgrounds

### Pure White
**Hex:** `#FFFFFF`
**Usage:** Card backgrounds, main content areas

## Usage Examples

- **Navigation borders:** Ultra Light Gray (`#E8E8ED`)
- **Card borders:** Light Gray (`#D2D2D7`)
- **Card hover borders:** Off-black (`#1D1D1F`)
- **Icon color:** Micro-blue (`#0078D4`)
- **Heading text:** Off-black (`#1D1D1F`)
- **Body text:** Medium Gray (`#6E6E73`)
- **Button background:** Micro-blue (`#0078D4`)
